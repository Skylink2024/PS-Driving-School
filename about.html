About the instructor

Your Driving Journey Starts Here.

Amazinglee Driving Academy offers Code 8 (manual and automatic) and Code 10 driving lessons in Cape Town Southern Suburbs, including Mitchell’s Plain Town Centre. The school is based in Taranto, postal code 7798.

Published packages cover lesson hours alone, or hours with car or truck hire for the test. The focus is clear instruction and helping learners build confidence on the road. Terms and conditions apply.
