About Amazinglee

Your Driving Journey Starts Here.

Amazinglee Driving Academy offers Code 8 (manual and automatic) and Code 10 driving lessons in Cape Town Southern Suburbs, including Mitchell’s Plain Town Centre. Based in Taranto (7798), the school focuses on clear instruction and helping learners build real confidence on the road.

Packages cover lesson hours alone, or hours with car or truck hire for the test. Amazinglee is a BEE Level 1 contributor and a SADSOA member. Terms and conditions apply.
