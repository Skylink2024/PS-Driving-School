About Amazinglee

Your Driving Journey Starts Here.

Like other Cape Town driving schools, Amazinglee covers the practical side of getting licensed: Code 8 lessons in manual and automatic, Code 10 lessons, and the option to hire the school vehicle for the driving test.

Lessons are based in Taranto (7798) and also serve Cape Town Southern Suburbs and Mitchell’s Plain Town Centre. Amazinglee is a BEE Level 1 contributor and a SADSOA member. Terms and conditions apply.
